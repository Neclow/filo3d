# Filo3D

A searchable database of high-resolution **Filoviridae** protein structures from the
[RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org) — a filovirus counterpart to
[CoV3D](https://cov3d.ibbr.umd.edu). Covers the full proteome (**NP, VP35, VP40, GP,
VP30, VP24, L**) across *Ebolavirus*, *Marburgvirus*, *Cuevavirus* (Lloviu) and
*Dianlovirus* (Měnglà).

Search across every field (try `GP`, `Marburg`, a PDB id, or `Yes`), sort any column,
and click a PDB id to open it at RCSB.
